TITLE OF INVENTION

Immersion-Cooled Battery Module with Dielectric Fluid Circulation

ABSTRACT

A battery module houses cylindrical cells fully immersed in a non-conductive dielectric fluid inside a sealed enclosure. A circulation pump moves the fluid past the cells and through an external plate heat exchanger. A level sensor detects fluid loss, and the module disconnects the cells from the load when the fluid level drops below a minimum mark.

TECHNICAL FIELD

The present disclosure relates to cooling of high-power battery modules for stationary energy storage.

BACKGROUND

Cold plates cool battery cells only through one face, which leaves large temperature differences across each cell during fast charging. Direct contact between the cells and a coolant removes heat more evenly, but water-based coolants conduct electricity and cannot touch the cell terminals.

SUMMARY

According to one aspect, a battery module comprises a sealed enclosure filled with a dielectric fluid, a plurality of cylindrical cells immersed in the fluid, a circulation pump, and a heat exchanger outside the enclosure. Because the fluid is electrically insulating, it may contact the cell terminals directly.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

[0020] The enclosure 200 holds forty-eight cylindrical cells 202 standing upright in a polymer holder. The enclosure is filled with a synthetic ester dielectric fluid having a breakdown voltage above 60 kV.

[0021] A circulation pump 210 draws warm fluid from the top of the enclosure and pushes it through a plate heat exchanger 220, where it transfers heat to a building water loop, before returning it to the bottom of the enclosure.

[0022] A float-type level sensor 230 monitors the fluid level. If the level falls below a minimum mark, the battery management unit opens a contactor 240 that disconnects the cells from the load.

[0023] The pump runs at a fixed speed whenever the module is charging or discharging; no per-cell temperature estimation is performed.

CLAIMS

What is claimed is:

1. A battery module comprising: a sealed enclosure; a dielectric fluid filling the enclosure; a plurality of cylindrical battery cells immersed in the dielectric fluid; a circulation pump configured to move the dielectric fluid through a heat exchanger located outside the enclosure.

2. The battery module of claim 1, wherein the dielectric fluid is a synthetic ester having a breakdown voltage above 60 kV.

3. The battery module of claim 1, further comprising a level sensor and a contactor, wherein the contactor disconnects the cells from a load when the level sensor detects a fluid level below a minimum mark.
